◆ READING THE INSTRUMENTS

FINALIZED
Reconciliation verified the canonical observation.
PROVISIONAL
Observed at a confirmed or processed commitment; reconciliation is pending.
NOT READ
The requested evidence has not been recorded.
STALE
The observation or evidence is older than its freshness window.
CHANGED
The finalized record differs, or the observation was reorged.
